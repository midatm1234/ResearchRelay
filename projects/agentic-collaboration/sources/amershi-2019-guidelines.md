# Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction

## Findings

The authors organize practical interaction guidance around introducing AI, routine use, failures, and longer-term adaptation.[^amershi-2019-guidelines]

## Methods

Iterative synthesis and evaluation, including 49 design practitioners assessing 20 AI-enabled products.[^amershi-2019-guidelines]

## Limitations

Evaluates guideline relevance, rather than causal productivity gains or asynchronous scientific agents.

## Implications for ResearchRelay — design inference

Expose capabilities, uncertainty, recent changes, correction paths, and recoverable failures; ask participants whether handoffs are understandable.
