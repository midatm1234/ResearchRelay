# When combinations of humans and AI are useful: A systematic review and meta-analysis

## Findings

Combining people and AI did not consistently beat the stronger component; task type and relative component ability mattered.[^vaccaro-2024-human-ai]

## Methods

Preregistered meta-analysis: 106 experiments and 370 effect sizes, with human-only, AI-only, and combined conditions.[^vaccaro-2024-human-ai]

## Limitations

The included studies end in June 2023 and vary in design; this is not a direct test of current agentic research teams.

## Implications for ResearchRelay — design inference

Measure coordination effort and research quality against human-only and agent-only baselines; do not assume collaboration creates synergy.
